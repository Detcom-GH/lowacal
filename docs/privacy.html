Lowacal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Lowacal is a desktop calendar application that runs on your own computer. The developer operates no servers for it and receives none of your data. This page explains what the application does with the data it handles.

Data Lowacal accesses

When you add an account, Lowacal accesses, from the provider you chose (Google Calendar, iCloud or another CalDAV server):

  • the list of your calendars, with their names and colours;
  • the events in those calendars (title, time, location, description);
  • for a Google account, your email address, which is used only to label the account inside the app.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the features you see in the app: showing your calendars and events, showing reminders for upcoming events, and creating, changing or deleting events when you ask the app to.

Where the data is stored

  • A copy of your calendars and events is kept in a local database on your computer, so the app can start quickly and show reminders.
  • Sign-in credentials (the Google access authorisation, or the password you entered for iCloud/CalDAV) are kept in your system keyring (KWallet or the Secret Service keyring), not in plain files.

Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Sharing

Lowacal does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. Your computer talks directly to your calendar provider and to no one else. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Google user data

Lowacal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to develop or train AI or machine-learning models, and is not read by any person other than you.

Deleting your data

  • Removing an account in Lowacal deletes its cached calendars and events from your computer and removes its credentials from the keyring.
  • You can withdraw Lowacal's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions can be asked through the issue tracker.

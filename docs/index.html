Lowacal

A free, minimal desktop calendar for Linux

Lowacal is an open-source calendar application for the Linux desktop. It shows the calendars you already have in Google Calendar, iCloud and any CalDAV server (Nextcloud, Fastmail and others) in one window, and reminds you about upcoming events.

What it does

  • Month, week and day views of all your calendars together
  • Create, edit and delete events; changes are saved back to your calendar provider
  • Reminders as desktop notifications, also while the window is closed (the app stays in the system tray)
  • Add an account in a few clicks, without editing configuration files

How Lowacal uses your Google data

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, Lowacal asks for access to your Google Calendar. It uses that access only to read your calendars and events so it can display them and remind you about them, and to create, change or delete events when you do so in the app.

Lowacal runs entirely on your computer. Your calendar data travels directly between your computer and Google; it is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
